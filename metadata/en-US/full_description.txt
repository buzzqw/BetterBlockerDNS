BetterBlockerDNS (BBDNS) is a free, open-source DNS ad blocker for Android. It blocks ads, trackers, malware, phishing and unwanted content system-wide through a local VPN — no root required and no data leaves your device.

✓ Native HaGeZi and StevenBlack blocklist catalogues
✓ One-tap protection profiles (Essential, Balanced, Pro, Aggressive, Maximum)
✓ Compatibility allowlist for banking, payments, public administration and app stores
✓ DNS ad & tracker blocking
✓ Custom and pre-made blocklists
✓ Automatic blocklist updates
✓ Per-app firewall rules
✓ DNS request logging
✓ Material 3 design

🚫 DNS AD BLOCKING & FILTERING

Stop ads and trackers before they load with powerful DNS filtering, system-wide and inside apps.

- Native support for all HaGeZi lists: Multi LIGHT / NORMAL / PRO / PRO++ / ULTIMATE, Threat Intelligence Feeds, Fake, Pop-Up Ads, DynDNS, Badware Hoster, DoH/VPN bypass, Native Trackers, NSFW, Gambling, Social and more
- Native StevenBlack unified hosts file and fakenews / gambling / porn / social extensions
- One-tap protection profiles
- Custom blocklists: hosts, ABP, DNSMasq, wildcard and regex formats
- Automatic blocklist updates from 6 hours to 1 week
- DNS over HTTPS (RFC 8484) with Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, AdGuard, DNS.SB, Control D, DNSBunker, DNS4EU and custom endpoints
- Free plain DNS resolvers: Google, Cloudflare (standard, malware, family), Quad9, AdGuard (standard, family), OpenDNS, CleanBrowsing, DNS.SB, Control D, DNS4EU and Yandex
- Custom DNS servers
- Block specific domains manually
- Simple or detailed DNS request logging in real time

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST

- Everything runs locally on your device
- No analytics, no telemetry, no external account
- No root and no system partition changes
- VPN-based filtering only: no root and no privileged helper modes

🎨 MODERN DESIGN

- Material 3 with light, dark and dynamic colours
- Quick Settings tile
- Per-app Wi-Fi and cellular controls
- Backup and restore of settings and lists
